Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 15, 2026

1. What We Collect

  • Event listings: Title, description, date, time, location, category, and contact email (if provided) when you post an event.
  • RSVP emails: Email address and the event you RSVP'd to, stored in our database.
  • Location: GPS coordinates (browser permission required) — used only to sort nearby events. Never stored on our servers.
  • Local storage: Your saved events, notification preferences, and organiser profile are stored in your browser's localStorage. We cannot access this data.

2. How We Use Data

  • Event listings are publicly visible in the app feed.
  • RSVP emails are used only to send one event reminder email per RSVP. We do not sell email addresses.
  • Aggregate usage data (event views, going counts) may be used to improve the product.

3. Third-Party Services

  • Supabase — Database hosting (US). Privacy policy: supabase.com/privacy
  • Gumroad — Payment processing for paid tiers. Privacy policy: gumroad.com/privacy
  • Nominatim / OpenStreetMap — Geocoding event addresses. Privacy policy: osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy

4. Data Retention

Event listings are retained until removed by the organiser or Evently. RSVP email records are deleted 30 days after the event date.

5. Your Rights

You may request deletion of any data associated with your email address by contacting us at hello@evently.app. We will respond within 7 business days.

6. Cookies

We do not use tracking cookies. We use browser localStorage for user preferences only.

7. Children

Evently is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.
